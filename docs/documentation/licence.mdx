# Licence Manager

To ensure a seamless, secure, and easily updatable experience, my assets use a custom License Manager system. 

When you purchase an asset from my stores (Jinxxy or Booth), you will initially download the License Manager tool instead of the asset itself. This tool allows you to sync your account, verify your purchases, and download your assets directly inside Unity.

Here is a complete step-by-step guide on how to set it up.

---

## 1. Opening the License Manager

After you download the License Manager from the store and import the `.unitypackage` into your Unity project:

1. Look at the top menu bar in Unity.
2. Click on **CrZVRC** > **License Manager**.
3. The License Manager window will open and dock into your Unity layout.

---

## 2. Syncing Your Account (One-Time Setup)

Before you can activate and download your assets, you need to sync your VRChat account with my plataform. **You only have to do this once.**

:::caution 
You **must** be logged into the **VRChat SDK** control panel within your Unity project. The License Manager securely and automatically extracts your VRChat User ID directly from the SDK.
:::

**Steps to Sync:**

1. **Check VRChat ID:** The License Manager will automatically fetch and display your VRChat User ID.
2. **Enter Email:** Type your preferred email address into the provided text field.
3. **Request Code:** Click the **Send Code** button. 
4. **Verify:** Check your email inbox for the verification code. Enter this code into the License Manager and click **Verify code**.

:::info 
My platform only stores your **Email address** and your public **VRChat ID**. No passwords or sensitive information are ever collected or stored.
:::

---

## 3. Registering and Downloading Assets

### Obtaining Your License Key

*   **Jinxxy Purchases:** Your license key is provided automatically upon purchase on the store page or in your receipt.
*   **Booth Purchases:** Booth does not generate license keys automatically. To get your key, please join our [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/3W7h3Bp3mH), open a support ticket, and provide proof of your purchase. I will manually generate and send your license key.

### Activating Your Asset

1. **Register the License:** Enter your obtained license key into the License Manager and submit it.
2. **View Activated Products:** Once registered, your product will immediately appear in the **Activated Products** list inside the License Manager window.
3. **Download & Import:** Click the download button next to your asset. The License Manager will download the asset directly into your project and automatically open the standard Unity dialog prompting you to import the `.unitypackage`.

You are all set! The asset is now in your project and ready to be used.

---


:::danger
Please be aware that your licenses are permanently linked to the **VRChat User ID** you used during the synchronization process. 

Because of this direct link, **if you lose access to your VRChat account for any reason (e.g., losing your login credentials, account deletion, or bans), you will also lose access to your purchased assets.** 

Please ensure you keep your VRChat account secure, as licenses cannot be transferred to a different VRChat account once they are registered.
:::
