# Setup

Setting up the Room System in your VRChat world is quick and straightforward. Follow these steps to place the rooms in your world.

---
1. **Import the Package:** Ensure you have successfully imported the Room System Unity package into your project.
2. **Place Your Rooms:** Locate the **Room System** prefab in your project files. Drag and drop this prefab into your scene hierarchy. You need to do this for *every* room you want to create (e.g., if your world has 5 separate rooms, drag the prefab into the scene 5 times and position them accordingly).
3. **Add the Rooms Manager:** Next, locate the **Rooms Management** prefab and drag a single instance of it into your scene. This prefab acts as the brain that coordinates all the individual rooms you just placed.
4. **Link the Rooms to the Manager:** Select the **Rooms Management** prefab in your scene hierarchy. In the Unity Inspector, look for its Udon script. Drag and drop every single **Room System** prefab from your scene into the rooms array on this script. 

    **Important:** Add the rooms in a logical, sequential order (for example, starting from the first room in a corridor and ending with the last). The system automatically assigns the room numbers based on the exact order they are placed in this list.

5. **Open the CrZVRC Tool:** Look at the very top toolbar of your Unity editor. Click on **CrZVRC** and select **Room System** from the dropdown menu.
6. **Generate Render Textures:** A custom editor window will open. Simply click the **Generate Render Textures** button. 
7. **Wait for Confirmation:** The tool will automatically configure the necessary render textures for your UI. Once it finishes, a small popup window will appear notifying you that the setup is complete!
